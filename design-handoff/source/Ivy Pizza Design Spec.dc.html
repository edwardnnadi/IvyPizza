08 · Mobile (375–390px), live

The real page running at phone width. Scroll inside the frame: nav collapses, experience stacks, the basket becomes a floating bar that opens a bottom sheet.

Nav: logo, Menu, Account. Solidifies with blur after 30px of scroll.

Basket bar: appears after 70% of the hero. Empty: "Start your order → Menu". Filled: count + subtotal → opens sheet. Fly-to-basket targets the bar.

Sheet: 88vh max, drag handle, close button, Esc and scrim to dismiss; body scroll locks while open.

Experience: photo top, copy below, same pinned scrub.

Data: hero ≤ 180 KB AVIF/WebP at 1280w with 640w srcset; other photos ≤ 120 KB, lazy-loaded. Fonts ≈ 60 KB total.
